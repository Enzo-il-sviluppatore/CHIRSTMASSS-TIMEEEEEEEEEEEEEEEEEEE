# Music files

The audio-only player expects these six files in this folder:

1. 01-your-youtube-pick.mp3
2. 02-your-youtube-pick.mp3
3. 03-your-youtube-pick.mp3
4. 04-joy-to-the-world.mp3
5. 05-silent-night.mp3
6. 06-god-rest-ye-merry-gentlemen.mp3

Only upload audio recordings you made, have permission to use, or whose license explicitly permits redistribution and hosting on a public website. A traditional carol may be public domain while a newer recording can still be copyrighted. Do not download or extract audio from a YouTube video unless its creator/rightsholder expressly permits that use.

The site attempts audible autoplay. Most browsers block sound until a visitor interacts with the page; if that happens, the first tap/click retries playback and the Play button is available.
